- Lemon palette: `--lemon-50 #fffdf0`, `--lemon-100 #fff7c7`, `--lemon-200 #ffeba0`, `--lemon-300 #f9db69`, `--lemon-400 #f5ca42`, `--lemon-500 #eeb424`, `--lemon-700 #9b6d08`.
- Leaf green is the supporting color; cards use warm white backgrounds, rounded corners, and light shadows.
- The UI is mobile-first with content-width constraints. Navigation uses circular icon buttons and a bottom navigation rail on small viewports.
- Typography uses the app's system sans-serif stack, heavy compact headings, and uppercase eyebrow labels.
